Current and Proposed Formats

How it works now:

The current College Football Playoff Format (2024-2027) includes 12 seeds, which are chosen as follows:

  • Guaranteed bids for the top five conference champions (All P4 teams and 1 G6 team)
  • At-large bids for the seven highest-ranked remaining teams
  • If Notre Dame is in the top 12, it is guaranteed a bid as well
